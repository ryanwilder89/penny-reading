# Penny's Reading Practice: Evidence-Based Phonics Curriculum

This document contains the complete lesson content, learning materials, and scope and sequence used by Penny's Reading Practice. It is structured to serve as a reference for creating similar age-appropriate reading content based on the Science of Reading (SoR) and evidence-based curricula.

## 1. Curriculum Overview
The curriculum follows a **Structured Literacy** approach, moving from simple sound-letter correspondences to complex vowel teams and morphological suffixes.

### Evidence-Based Foundations
The scope and sequence is derived from established reading programs such as **UFLI Foundations**, **CKLA**, and **Orton-Gillingham** principles. It prioritizes:
- **Explicit Instruction**: Direct teaching of phonics patterns.
- **Systematic Progression**: Moving from simple CVC words to multisyllabic and complex patterns.
- **Decodable Texts**: Practice using only the patterns previously taught.
- **Phonemic Awareness**: Auditory drills to build sound manipulation skills.

These passages are controlled to ensure students only encounter words they can decode based on the patterns they have learned.

| Title | Skill Target | Text Content |
|:---|:---|:---|

## 4. Interactive Exercises

### Word Chains (Orthographic Mapping)
Students change one sound at a time to build awareness of phoneme manipulation.
- `flat` → `flap` → `flip` → `clip` → `slip` → `slop` → `stop`
- `cap` → `cape` → `tape` → `tap` → `tip` → `time` → `lime`
- `ran` → `rain` → `pain` → `pan` → `pat` → `pay` → `say`
- `cat` → `cart` → `part` → `park` → `pork` → `fork`

### Phonemic Awareness Drills
Auditory-only exercises performed at the start of each session.
- **Deletion**: "Say 'stop'. Now say it without the /s/." (Answer: "top")
- **Substitution**: "Say 'flat'. Change /f/ to /s/." (Answer: "slat")

---

## 5. Learning Methodology
1. **Warmup**: Phonemic awareness (auditory only).
2. **Review**: Flashcard practice of previously mastered patterns (Spaced Repetition).
3. **Practice**: Word building and word chains (encoding/decoding).
4. **Read**: Application of skills in a decodable passage (fluency).
